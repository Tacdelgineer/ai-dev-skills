Imagine you hire a hundred AI agents. They never sleep, and they have one job: build me a website.
Here's what happens.
All one hundred start on the homepage at once. Half paint it orange, half paint it blue, and they keep painting over each other.
Twelve are still researching what a website is. And one is building a calculator nobody asked for.
Meanwhile, your bill climbs, because every agent costs money each time it thinks. When Anthropic tested its own multi-agent research system, it used about fifteen times more tokens than a normal chat.
So you make one agent the manager. It splits the job into pieces, gives each team one piece, and puts a checker at the end.
Suddenly <break time="0.6s" /> it's quiet.
By the afternoon, you have a website.
The hard part was never the agents. It was the plan.
