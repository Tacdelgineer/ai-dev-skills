Two AIs get the same job: book dinner for six on Friday, under thirty dollars each.
The first is a chatbot with no tools. In two seconds, it writes a beautiful list of five restaurants. Done!
Except <break time="0.6s" /> it didn't book anything. It can't. And two of those places closed last year.
The second is an agent, with tools and permission to use them. It's slower. It finds places open Friday, checks the prices, and tries to book the best one.
The table's full.
So it tries its second choice, which has a table at seven. It books it, adds it to your calendar, and texts your friends.
The real difference is tools. Without them, an AI can only tell you what to do. With them, it can do it, check the result, and fix what goes wrong.
That's also why agents cost more, and why you should still check their work.
