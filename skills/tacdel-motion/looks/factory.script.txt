Halfway through a long chat, you mention one thing that matters: you're vegetarian.
An hour later, your AI plans you a dinner <break time="0.6s" /> at a steakhouse.
So what happened? Let's look inside.
Every message becomes a box on a workbench. The bench is everything the AI can see at once, and it only fits so much.
As the chat grows, your box ends up in the middle. It's still there, but researchers found models use the start and end best, so the middle gets overlooked.
When the bench is full, boxes actually leave. Some apps drop the oldest. Some squash them into a summary, and details fall out. Some move them to storage, and fetch the right one when needed.
That's how your box comes back.
